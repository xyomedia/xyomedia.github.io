China-Europe Film & Media

中欧影视制作和交流平台

Based in France and bridging China and Europe, XYO Media France is an international film and television production company specializing in cross-border production and collaboration for feature films, series, TV programs, and commercials. The company provides full production services in Europe for Chinese film crews, including pre-production, on-set assistance, shoot supervision, and post-production, with extensive experience in cross-cultural projects.

立足法国，连接中国与欧洲，笑游文化传媒 (XYO Media)是一家国际影视制作公司，专注于长片电影、剧集、电视节目及广告的跨境制作与合作。

XYO MEDIA Gallery 作品展示

XYO MEDIA brings together Sino-European cross-border production expertise, specializing in full-process filmmaking from creative planning and script development to directorial guidance. The company’s works span short films, feature films, TV series, documentaries, and commercials, including the short films Drifting Lives, Boutique de Tofu at Midnight, and the cultural short film series Tan Xiao Feng Sheng, as well as feature films such as Last Resort, CINEPHILES, HAPPY NIGHT, and the documentary THE STORY OF CHINA. In co-production, the company is currently preparing the Sino-European feature film The Wind and the Light and the short film collection Histoires Fantastiques Revisitées. Additionally, XYO MEDIA has contributed to post-production projects such as KIM KONG, THE HISTORY OF LOVE, and LUCY, and has coordinated promotion and distribution for multiple films and commercials, including FOX HUNTER, PUFFER RHAPSODY, and 100 Île-de-France.

XYO MEDIA 汇聚中欧跨境制片经验，专注于从创意策划、剧本开发到导演指导的全流程制作。公司作品涵盖短片、长片、电视剧、纪录片及广告等多种类型，包括短片《Drifting Lives》（流动的人）、《Boutique de Tofu à Minuit》（深夜豆腐店）、文化短片系列《坦笑风生》，以及长片《Last Resort》（终极比率）、《CINEPHILES》、《HAPPY NIGHT》与纪录片《THE STORY OF CHINA》（中国故事）。在联合制片方面，公司正筹备中欧联合制片长片《The Wind and the Light》（风与光）及系列短片《Histoires Fantastiques Revisitées》（大话聊斋）。此外，XYO MEDIA 曾参与后期制作项目如《KIM KONG》、《THE HISTORY OF LOVE》和《LUCY》，并承担多部影片及广告的宣发发行协调，包括《FOX HUNTER》、《PUFFER RHAPSODY》和《100 Île-de-France》。
