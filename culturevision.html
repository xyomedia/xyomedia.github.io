Culture Vision 《文化视界》

“Culture Vision” — A professional cultural magazine exploring film, fashion, art, publishing, music, and global cultural creativity. As a professional cultural media platform, we focus on the Cannes Film Festival, Paris Fashion Week, international art exhibitions, book fairs, and music festivals, highlighting global cultural events and creative trends.

《文化视界》——专业文化杂志，涵盖电影、时尚、艺术、出版与图书、音乐以及全球文化创意领域。作为专业文化媒体，我们聚焦戛纳电影节、巴黎时装周、国际艺术展、图书展及音乐节现场，关注全球文化事件与创意趋势。

Cannes Film Festival Report | 戛纳报道

Focus on the 78th Cannes Film Festival, featuring official news and red carpet highlights. Selected films and directors offer a glimpse into cinema art and Chinese-language films on the global stage.

聚焦第78届戛纳电影节，呈现官方资讯与红毯亮点。通过精选影片与导演，展现电影艺术与华语电影的国际舞台。

Portrait | 人物

Profiles are primarily drawn from the film and fashion industries, as well as the cultural and creative sectors and notable public figures. Geographic representation focuses mainly on Europe, North America, and Asia, while also including other regions.

人物来源主要涵盖 电影与时尚行业，以及文化创意领域与社会名流。地区分布以 欧美与亚洲为主，并覆盖其他地区。

Cinema | 电影

Focusing on the scale and awards ecosystem of global cinema: 5,000–7,000 films produced annually, 3,000+ film festivals worldwide, including 50–70 major international festivals, with 300–500 award-winning films each year.

聚焦全球电影产业规模与荣誉体系：5000–7000 部年度电影制作、3000+ 电影节、50–70 个主要国际电影节及每年 300–500 部获奖影片。

Paris Fashion Week Feature | 时装周

Paris Fashion Week began in 1973. Each season, beyond 100+ official shows, the city hosts numerous independent events, bringing the total to over 1,000 fashion activities.

巴黎时装周始于 1973 年。每一季除 100 余场官方走秀 外，巴黎城内还会举办大量独立活动，使时尚相关活动总数 超过 1000 场。

Fashion | 时尚

Focusing on global fashion brands and industry trends: 5,000+ active brands, 400+ major runway shows each year, and evolving design and consumer trends.

聚焦全球时尚产业与品牌趋势：5000+ 活跃品牌、每年 400+ 场主要时装秀，以及年度设计与消费趋势变化。

Art | 艺术

Focusing on the global art ecosystem: 300+ international art fairs each year, including around 30–40 leading global fairs, alongside thousands of international exhibitions worldwide.

聚焦全球艺术生态：全球每年 300+ 国际艺术博览会，其中约 30–40 个为最具影响力的艺术展，并涵盖数千场国际艺术展览与重要展出。
