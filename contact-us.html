Work With Us

Based in France and connecting China with Europe,

XYO Media is an international production company dedicated to cross-border film and media collaboration.

We focus on the core creative strengths of filmmaking—creative development, scriptwriting, and director collaboration,

while also providing seamless Sino-European production coordination and execution.

XYO Media works closely with a network of renowned directors,

supporting their projects across both production and post-production stages with professional expertise and integrated resources.

Through these collaborations, we have delivered high-quality films, series, and commercial projects,

ensuring full-process management from shooting through to distribution.

Project Types

Feature Films | Short Films | Series | TV Programs | Commercials

At the intersection of cultures and storytelling, we bring ideas to life and connect them with global audiences.

XYO Media — Bringing stories across borders to the world.
